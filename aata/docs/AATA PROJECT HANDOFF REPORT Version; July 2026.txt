Project Overview
AATA (AIMn Autonomous Trading Academy) is an AI-driven educational platform designed to teach trading through a team of specialized AI Professors rather than a single AI assistant.
The Academy is built as a modular architecture where each Professor has a specific area of expertise but collaborates with the others.
The goal is to create an expandable AI educational system that can eventually train traders, improve itself, and continuously expand its knowledge.

Overall Architecture
The Academy consists of multiple specialized AI Professors.
Current Professors include:
    • Trading Professor 
    • Risk Professor 
    • Psychology Professor 
    • Market Structure Professor 
    • Vision Professor 
Future Professors will follow the same implementation template.

Design Philosophy
Every AI Professor:
    • teaches 
    • analyzes 
    • collaborates 
    • generates lessons 
    • submits new Academy rules 
    • continuously improves 
    • follows evidence-based decision making 
    • records important knowledge 
    • maintains confidence scoring 
    • communicates with other Professors 
No Professor acts independently when another Professor has relevant expertise.
