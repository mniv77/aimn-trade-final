# AATA Design Notes

## Living System Architecture

AATA should be designed like a living organism rather than a collection of independent AI agents.

Every Professor is analogous to an organ in the human body.

Each has a unique responsibility.

Some responsibilities intentionally overlap to provide validation and resilience.

Examples:

- Trading Professor → Brain (Decision Making)
- Vision Professor → Eyes (Observation)
- Risk Professor → Heart (Capital Protection)
- Market Structure Professor → Spatial Awareness (Market Orientation)
- Psychology Professor → Learning and Adaptation
- Professor Architecture → Skeleton (Structural Framework)
- Communication Layer → Circulatory System
- Data Acquisition → Lungs
- Academy Principles → DNA

The Academy succeeds because all Professors cooperate as one integrated intelligent system rather than operating independently.

Redundancy is intentional and increases reliability, just as the human body has two eyes, two ears, two lungs, and two kidneys.
