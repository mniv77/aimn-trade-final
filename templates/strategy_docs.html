📊 1. RSI Real Mathematical Engine & Rules

Unlike standard Wilder Relative Strength Index calculations which rely on smoothed moving averages of gains and losses, the RSI Real engine evaluates historical price boundaries directly over a strict lookback period (default: 14 intervals).

Lookback Window = 14
Max Price Bounds = 100 Units | Min Price Bounds = 0 Units
Normalization Formula: Maps current actual asset price relative to historical extremes within the active timeframe window.

Purpose: Eliminates lagging indicator distortion during high-volatility cryptocurrency moves on BTC/USD, ETH/USD, and SOL/USD.

🎯 5. The Strategy: Pure Trend-Change, Reverse-Trailing & Database State Engine

The absolute heart of the AiMn trading system is structural alignment: We enter when the trend changes, and we exit when the trend changes. Under normal operations, trades never enter prolonged losing territory because exits are triggered immediately upon structural trend reversal.

A. Entry via Reverse-Trailing Lookback

To eliminate lagging entries (such as catching a price drop 25 minutes too late), the engine performs a historical backward scan on every inspection loop. It scans 10 to 14 candles back, applies the trailing profit indicator backwards across those bars, and pinpoints the exact pivot point where the trailing "minus %" buffer was triggered—giving us an instant, lag-free entry timestamp (ctime).

B. Database State Memory (Tracking Previous Trend & Time)

To prevent redundant calculations, the database permanently logs key transition parameters: stored timestamp (ctime) for the last trend change and active trend state (LONG/SHORT bias). During each check, current momentum is compared against the database state to ignore noise and execute state flips instantly.

C. Exit via Trailing Peak Take-Profit

Exits mirror our entry philosophy: the system locks in the absolute highest price peak achieved during the trade's lifecycle. When price retraces by the predefined negative offset percentage (minus %) from that peak, it signals a structural trend change and automatically exits the trade.

D. Noise Management & Danger Protection

Multi-candle threshold filters prevent minor market noise from triggering false reversals. If a noise entry opens incorrectly and expands adversely, the RSI Real Circuit Breaker (forcing emergency market exits on extreme values like RSI Real < 15 for longs or > 85 for shorts) acts as our ultimate safety net.
